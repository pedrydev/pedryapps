# Notifications

The Notifications mini-app collects in-app messages posted by mini-apps and shows their unread count in the app's menu bar. Back to [GLOSSARY-MAP.md](../../GLOSSARY-MAP.md).

## Language

**Notification**:
A message posted by a mini-app into the app's in-app inbox. It records what happened and leaves the Inbox six hours after it was posted.
_Avoid_: Alert, toast, banner, system notification

**Inbox**:
The Notifications mini-app's collection of live Notifications.
_Avoid_: Feed, log, notification center

**Notification type**:
Whether a Notification is Information or an Attention request, used to highlight its importance and filter the Inbox. Reading marks any Notification read.
_Avoid_: Severity, priority, category

**Information**:
A Notification type for an event that has already happened and may be worth checking now or later.
_Avoid_: Info, FYI

**Attention request**:
A Notification type for an event that happened and needs further action from the user. It is highlighted more strongly than Information.
_Avoid_: Action required, urgent, alert

**Unread count**:
The number of Notifications in the Inbox that have not been read, shown as a badge on the app's existing menu bar item.
_Avoid_: Badge number, notification total
